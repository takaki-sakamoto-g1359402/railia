## Naming, side, hierarchy, and canvas conventions

- PSD root group: `riai`.
- Leaf layer names use unique ASCII `snake_case` and match `^[a-z][a-z0-9_]*$`.
- `l` and `r` mean Riai's anatomical left and right, never the viewer's left and right.
- The hair ornament is provisionally named on Riai's right because that is the placement shown in the primary front-oriented presentation. If the approved redraw resolves a genuine contradiction, rename the affected layers and record the decision; do not silently mirror the design.
- Authoring canvas: 4096 x 4096 px, 8-bit sRGB, transparent. Fit the approved upper-body silhouette with at least 12.5% clear margin around the maximum expected deformation envelope. If the approved crop cannot meet this, revise the canvas before painting and record the new size.
- All leaf layers retain the full canvas origin; never trim per-layer bounds in the PSD.
- Suggested PSD groups, back to front: `riai/01_tail_rear`, `riai/02_cloak_rear`, `riai/03_hair_ears_rear`, `riai/04_body_head_base`, `riai/05_face`, `riai/06_hair_front`, `riai/07_garment_arms_front`, `riai/08_accessories`, `riai/09_optional_fx`.
- Group names are organizational folders, not raster layers and not Cubism ArtMeshes.

## Global raster and import rules

Every inventory row below is a proposed raster leaf. Unless a row says otherwise:

- **Transparency** means transparent pixels outside the painted part on the shared full canvas; no white reference background.
- Extend paint under every overlap by the approved deformation envelope plus a minimum 24 px safety bleed at 4096 px authoring size. High-motion roots such as ears, hair, sleeves, and tail require larger artist-reviewed bleed.
- Import as one Cubism ArtMesh per raster leaf. Mesh density, deformer placement, and physics named below are proposals for later rigging, not completed work.
- Static constellation print, stitching, and local shading that never moves independently should stay baked into the owning garment/hair raster. Do not create hundreds of decorative ArtMeshes.
- Use clipping in Cubism only where the row calls for it. Do not rely on PSD clipping masks or layer effects in the approved import copy.

2. Redraw the strict front-neutral face and body; do not treat the angled presentation as an orthographic front view.

8. Approve the hair-ornament side and all chain/charm attachment points where panels conflict or hide the connection.
9. Approve every optional fine strand, charm, and glow. Omit unsupported details instead of balancing the design by invention.
